Expectations

Reliable, prepared, coachable

Reliable

If you commit to raid, we expect you to be there or communicate early when real life gets in the way.

Prepared

Know your class, understand the encounter and arrive ready to contribute. We do not expect perfection.

Team-first

Damage and healing matter, but mechanics, utility, attitude and helping the group succeed matter too.
