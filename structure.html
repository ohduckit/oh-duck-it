GUILD STRUCTURE

The Tavern Council

⌂Overview ♛Tavern Council ⚒Officer Roles ♜Guild Ranks ⚖Decision Making

Council-led. Clear responsibility.

Oh Duck It is governed collectively by the Tavern Council. Officers and activity leads own clearly defined areas so the people closest to a part of the guild can run it well without creating a top-heavy chain of command.

🍻 TAVERN COUNCIL

Collective governance, equal vote, guild-wide direction and consequential decisions.
